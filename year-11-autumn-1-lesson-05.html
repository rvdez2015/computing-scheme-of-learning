Planning draft. Check that the suggested example and assessment match this lesson title before teaching.

Learning and success criteria

  • Explain and apply the key idea in 1.5 - Performance.
  • Select or produce a valid solution, identify the relevant rule and justify it with evidence from the scenario.

Subject knowledge and resources

  • Create two worked examples for “1.5 - Performance”, including one plausible error. Label the decision or step that makes the difference.
  • Anticipated misconception: Pupils may recognise the vocabulary but omit the reason or apply the rule to the wrong case.
  • Prepare the worked example, a parallel pupil task and a model answer.

45-minute sequence

TimeTeaching and learning
0–5 minRetrieve one idea from 1.4 - Secondary Storage and connect it to today’s lesson.
5–15 minCreate two worked examples for “1.5 - Performance”, including one plausible error. Label the decision or step that makes the difference.
15–23 minWhich of the two proposed solutions best fits the scenario, and what evidence rules out the other? Expected evidence: Select or produce a valid solution, identify the relevant rule and justify it with evidence from the scenario. If pupils miss the key idea, re-model and ask a parallel question.
23–40 minGive a new scenario that changes one feature of the model. Ask pupils to apply the idea, show their working and correct an incorrect answer.
40–45 minAsk pupils to answer a fresh version of the check. Review their reasoning before planning the next lesson.

Adaptive teaching and challenge

  • Offer a partially worked model and a vocabulary reference where useful. Agree any individual adjustments before teaching.
  • Ask pupils to justify an alternative or test a boundary case using precise technical language.
