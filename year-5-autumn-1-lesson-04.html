L4. Identify input and output devices

Year 5 · Autumn 1 · 45 minutes

Back to Autumn 1Full-year plan Lesson observation form

Unit 1: Digital Safety

Learning objective

Classify buttons, sensors, screens and speakers using a micro:bit or labelled diagram.

Success criteria

Identify an input and an output and describe the direction of the information.

45-minute lesson sequence

TimeTeaching and learning
0–5 minRetrieve one idea from the previous lesson, “L3. Understand why passwords matter”, and connect it to today’s task.
5–15 minModel the first step of this activity with a fictional or teacher-made example. Classify buttons, sensors, screens and speakers using a micro:bit or labelled diagram.
15–23 minTry a second example together. Ask pupils to predict or justify a step. Look for this evidence: Identify an input and an output and describe the direction of the information.
23–40 minClassify buttons, sensors, screens and speakers using a micro:bit or labelled diagram.
40–45 minUse a new example or a short demonstration to check the success criterion. Ask pupils to explain their reasoning.

Preparation and adaptive teaching

Prepare a worked example, a parallel task and the materials needed for the activity. Offer a partially completed example, labelled visuals or a verbal response where appropriate. For extension, vary one requirement and ask pupils to explain its effect.

Targeted Teachers’ Standards

TS4 · Well-structured lessons

5–15 and 23–40 minutes. Model a first step, then check whether pupils can apply the same process to a fresh example independently.

TS6 · Assessment

15–23 and 40–45 minutes. Collect an explanation, worked example or demonstration that shows whether pupils can meet the criterion: Identify an input and an output and describe the direction of the information. Re-model the step if answers reveal a shared error.
