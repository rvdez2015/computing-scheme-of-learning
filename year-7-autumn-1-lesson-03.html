L3. Recognise phishing and fake content

Year 7 · Autumn 1 · 45 minutes

Back to Year 7 · Autumn 1

Unit 1: Online Safety

Planning draft. Check that the suggested example and assessment match this lesson title before teaching.

Learning and success criteria

  • Explain and apply the key idea in L3. Recognise phishing and fake content.
  • Identify a relevant indicator and explain a safe, proportionate response.

Subject knowledge and resources

  • Use fictional messages, profiles or incident cards. Model how to spot a risk and decide what to report through school channels.
  • Anticipated misconception: A familiar logo or sender name is not proof that a message is genuine.
  • Prepare the worked example, a parallel pupil task and a model answer.

45-minute sequence

TimeTeaching and learning
0–5 minRetrieve one idea from L2. Identify cyberbullying and how to report it and connect it to today’s lesson.
5–15 minUse fictional messages, profiles or incident cards. Model how to spot a risk and decide what to report through school channels.
15–23 minWhat evidence makes this situation risky, and what safe action should you take first? Expected evidence: Identify a relevant indicator and explain a safe, proportionate response. If pupils miss the key idea, re-model and ask a parallel question.
23–40 minClassify new scenarios, highlight the evidence and recommend a proportionate protective or reporting action.
40–45 minAsk pupils to answer a fresh version of the check. Review their reasoning before planning the next lesson.

Adaptive teaching and challenge

  • Offer a partially worked model and a vocabulary reference where useful. Agree any individual adjustments before teaching.
  • Ask pupils to predict what changes when one input, rule or design choice is varied.

Targeted Teachers’ Standards

TS3 · 5–15 min · Subject knowledge

Make the technical idea explicit and check the anticipated misconception. Look for accurate terminology and pupils correcting the error. Misconception: A familiar logo or sender name is not proof that a message is genuine.

TS6 · 15–23 and 40–45 min · Assessment

Compare the check and exit responses. Look for identify a relevant indicator and explain a safe, proportionate response. Record how the teacher responds to errors.

Post-lesson reflection

Which response showed that pupils met the success criteria? Which misconception needs another check? Record factual evidence and agree a next action in your school system.
