L1. Understand personal information and digital footprint

Year 7 · Autumn 1 · 45 minutes

Unit 1: Online Safety

Planning draft. Adapt resources and support for your class.

Learning and success criteria

  • Explain and apply the key idea in L1. Understand personal information and digital footprint.
  • Identify the combined evidence, explain the inference and propose an edit that prevents it.

Subject knowledge and resources

  • Compare two fictional profiles. One posts the school name and a regular travel time. Model how someone could combine those details to infer a routine.
  • Anticipated misconception: Private settings reduce the audience, but recipients can still copy or share a post.
  • Prepare the worked example, a parallel pupil task and a model answer.

45-minute sequence

TimeTeaching and learning
0–5 minElicit an example pupils already know and connect it to today’s lesson.
5–15 minCompare two fictional profiles. One posts the school name and a regular travel time. Model how someone could combine those details to infer a routine.
15–23 minHow can two individually ordinary details become identifying when combined? Expected evidence: Identify the combined evidence, explain the inference and propose an edit that prevents it. If pupils miss the key idea, re-model and ask a parallel question.
23–40 minAnnotate a fictional profile, remove two identifying details and explain how each edit reduces the risk.
40–45 minAsk pupils to answer a fresh version of the check. Review their reasoning before planning the next lesson.

Adaptive teaching and challenge

  • Offer a partially worked model and a vocabulary reference where useful. Agree any individual adjustments before teaching.
  • Ask pupils to predict what changes when one input, rule or design choice is varied.

Targeted Teachers’ Standards

TS3 · 5–15 min · Subject knowledge

Make the technical idea explicit and check the anticipated misconception. Look for accurate terminology and pupils correcting the error. Misconception: Private settings reduce the audience, but recipients can still copy or share a post.

TS6 · 15–23 and 40–45 min · Assessment

Compare the check and exit responses. Look for identify the combined evidence, explain the inference and propose an edit that prevents it. Record how the teacher responds to errors.

Post-lesson reflection

Which response showed that pupils met the success criteria? Which misconception needs another check? Record factual evidence and agree a next action in your school system.
