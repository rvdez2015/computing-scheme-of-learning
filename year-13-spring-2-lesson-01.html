Learning and success criteria

  • Explain and apply the key idea in Comp. Thinking Prodedurably.
  • Give an accurate point and link it to the facts of the scenario.

Subject knowledge and resources

  • Use a worked answer from the current unit and annotate where its marks come from.
  • Anticipated misconception: A technically correct term alone may not earn an explanation mark.
  • Prepare the worked example, a parallel pupil task and a model answer.

45-minute sequence

TimeTeaching and learning
0–5 minRetrieve one idea from Comp. Thinking Ahead and connect it to today’s lesson.
5–15 minUse a worked answer from the current unit and annotate where its marks come from.
15–23 minWhich part of the example earns the explanation mark, and why? Expected evidence: Give an accurate point and link it to the facts of the scenario. If pupils miss the key idea, re-model and ask a parallel question.
23–40 minAnswer two short questions and one application question based on the current unit. Compare with the model after submission.
40–45 minAsk pupils to answer a fresh version of the check. Review their reasoning before planning the next lesson.

Adaptive teaching and challenge

  • Offer a partially worked model and a vocabulary reference where useful. Agree any individual adjustments before teaching.
  • Ask pupils to justify an alternative or test a boundary case using precise technical language.
