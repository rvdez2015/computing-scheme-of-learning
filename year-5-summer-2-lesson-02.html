L2 – How Web Pages Work (HTML Basics)

Year 5 · Summer 2 · 45 minutes

Back to Summer 2Full-year plan

Developing for the Web

Learning objective

Change a heading and paragraph in a starter HTML file.

Success criteria

Match each edited element to what appears in the browser.

45-minute lesson sequence

TimeTeaching and learning
0–5 minRetrieve one idea from the previous lesson, “L1 – What Is a Website?”, and connect it to today’s task.
5–15 minModel the first step of this activity with a fictional or teacher-made example. Change a heading and paragraph in a starter HTML file.
15–23 minTry a second example together. Ask pupils to predict or justify a step. Look for this evidence: Match each edited element to what appears in the browser.
23–40 minChange a heading and paragraph in a starter HTML file.
40–45 minUse a new example or a short demonstration to check the success criterion. Ask pupils to explain their reasoning.

Preparation and adaptive teaching

Prepare a worked example, a parallel task and the materials needed for the activity. Offer a partially completed example, labelled visuals or a verbal response where appropriate. For extension, vary one requirement and ask pupils to explain its effect.

Targeted Teachers’ Standards

TS4 · Well-structured lessons

5–15 and 23–40 minutes. Model a first step, then check whether pupils can apply the same process to a fresh example independently.

TS6 · Assessment

15–23 and 40–45 minutes. Collect an explanation, worked example or demonstration that shows whether pupils can meet the criterion: Match each edited element to what appears in the browser. Re-model the step if answers reveal a shared error.

Reflection

Which response showed understanding? Which step needs another check next lesson?
