Learning and success criteria

  • Explain and apply the key idea in (a)Arithmetic, Boolean and assignment operators.
  • Trace the outcome accurately and use at least two meaningful cases to demonstrate the required behaviour.

Subject knowledge and resources

  • Use a short program that demonstrates the lesson feature. Predict a result, run it and trace the relevant variables or control flow.
  • Anticipated misconception: Code that runs once may still give the wrong output for a different input.
  • Prepare the worked example, a parallel pupil task and a model answer.
